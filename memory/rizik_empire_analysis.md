## Phase 2: Pivot to "Active Packaging" & Food Preservation
Leveraging the non-woven material expertise for high-tech food preservation, aiming to replace traditional aluminum foil and retort packs.

*   **Innovation:** A **Triple-Layer Sandwich** structure (Non-woven + LDPE Film + Non-woven).
*   **Mechanism:** The fabric acts as a "Scientific Filter", enabling breathing (crucial for vegetables) while simultaneously locking in moisture and oil (essential for spices and meat).
*   **The Retort Process:** Utilizing high-pressure steam (121°C) to sterilize food *inside* the pouch. 
*   **Impact:** This technology allows cooked meat to stay fresh for **1 year** at room temperature, completely eliminating the need for a refrigerator.
